The structural decision

The one choice that does most of the work

The paper form asks the enumerator to photocopy Section 4, hand-count eligible children at 3.02 from an "office use" column, and re-key each child's line number. The digital form doesn't ask any of that.

Paper design
  • Roster column (7) "Eligible for Section 4" — marked office use
  • 3.02 asks the enumerator to count column (7) live, in the field
  • 4.01 re-keys the child's line number by hand
  • Nothing checks that the count and the pages completed agree
hh2026_v1.xlsx
  • eligible_s4 computed the instant age is entered
  • 3.02 is not asked — there is nothing left to count
  • Child module nested inside the same roster row it belongs to
  • Eligible children = completed child modules, by construction

Read critically, not transcribed cleanly
