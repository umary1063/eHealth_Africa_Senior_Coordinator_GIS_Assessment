# Question 6: Building Capability in the Counterpart Agency

Compulsory, Part 3, maximum 6 pages excluding annexes.

**[Q6_Response.docx](Q6_Response.docx)** is the full response: interpretation of the capability
assessment evidence (the 0.11 self-rating/tested-knowledge correlation and the zero-of-21
software-access finding) and what each implies for course design and the binding constraint on
the whole programme; a four-level competency framework with observable behaviours per domain
(Annex A); a five-day course design with per-day learning outcomes, exercises, and an explicit
hands-on/instruction ratio; a stated scope decision on what is *not* taught and why; one 90-minute
session developed in full as a stand-alone facilitator artefact (Annex B); the pre/post practical
assessment instrument, scored on demonstrated capability rather than confidence (Annex C); and a
90-day post-course plan built around a transfer measure rather than a satisfaction measure.
